# Front CV Axle — Inspection, Removal and Installation (Ghost II)

## Inspection

Perform with the vehicle on the lift, wheels hanging free.

- Rotate each front wheel by hand and observe the axle shaft: visible runout
  or wobble at the inner tripod housing indicates internal wear.
- Inspect both boots (inner and outer) for splits, cracking in the bellows
  valleys, and thrown grease. Grease spray on the surrounding underbody is
  conclusive evidence of a breached boot; the joint must be replaced, not
  re-greased, if it has run dry under load.
- Grip the shaft adjacent to the inner joint and check for radial play. Any
  perceptible radial play at the tripod indicates wear beyond service limits.
- With an assistant applying light brake pressure, rock the wheel: a single
  metallic click per reversal from the outer joint region indicates outer
  joint wear.

Symptomatic correlation: inner-joint (tripod) wear produces a load-sensitive
shudder or vibration typically between 50 and 75 km/h; outer-joint wear
produces clicking on full-lock turns. See WSM-RR-2040 Stage 2 and
TSB-RR-2026-03.

## Removal

1. Remove the road wheel. Wheel bolts are single-use on this platform.
2. Release the hub nut with the vehicle on its wheels or with the brakes
   held; the nut is staked and single-use.
3. Remove the lower arm balljoint pinch bolt and separate the balljoint.
4. Support the axle and release the inner joint flange bolts from the final
   drive output flange. Discard the bolts — they are microencapsulated and
   single-use.
5. Withdraw the axle outboard first, protecting the boot bellows from the
   brake disc edge.

## Installation

1. Fit new inner flange bolts and torque to 62 Nm in a diagonal sequence.
2. Reconnect the balljoint; torque the pinch bolt to 47 Nm.
3. Fit a new hub nut. Torque to 320 Nm, then stake the collar into the shaft
   groove.
4. Fit the road wheel with new wheel bolts; torque to 140 Nm in a star
   pattern.
5. Road test per WSM-RR-1005 and confirm the original speed band is free of
   the symptom before RO completion.
