# TSB-RR-2026-03 — Ghost II Vibration 55–70 km/h

## Concern

Customer reports vibration or shudder felt through the floor and seat at
approximately 55–70 km/h, most noticeable around 60 km/h under light
acceleration. The symptom eases or disappears when coasting through the same
speed band. Wheel balancing does not resolve the concern.

## Affected vehicles

Ghost II vehicles from the production window September 2024 – November 2025.
The front axle shafts in this window were fitted with inner tripod joints
from a supplier batch exhibiting premature roller-track wear.

## Cause

Wear in the inner (plunge/tripod) joint of the front CV axle creates a
cyclic radial excitation under drive torque. Because the excitation is
torque-dependent, the vibration is strongest under light-to-moderate
acceleration in the 55–70 km/h band and fades on coast — distinguishing it
from wheel/tyre imbalance, which is load-insensitive and typically peaks at
higher road speed.

## Service action

1. Confirm the signature by road test per WSM-RR-1005 (load and coast
   discrimination, Step 2).
2. Inspect both front CV axles per WSM-RR-2041. Pay particular attention to
   radial play at the inner tripod housing; boot condition may be intact —
   an intact boot does not exclude this failure.
3. Where inner-joint play is found, replace the complete axle assembly per
   WSM-RR-2041. Replace in pairs only when both sides show play.
4. Do not attempt joint-only or grease-service repairs on affected axles.

## Warranty

Standard warranty terms apply within the affected production window. Quote
this bulletin number on the RO and record the road-test observations that
confirmed the load-sensitive signature.
