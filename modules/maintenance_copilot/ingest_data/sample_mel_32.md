# MEL 32-00 — General

The Minimum Equipment List (MEL) permits dispatch of the aircraft with specified
items inoperative, provided the associated operational (O) and maintenance (M)
procedures are applied. An aircraft may remain dispatchable even though each item
is individually relievable only under its listed conditions and time limits.

# MEL 32-31-01 — Antiskid System Inoperative

Category C. Dispatch permitted for up to 10 calendar days.

(O) Confirm the annunciation fault does not affect autobrake arming. Brief the
flight crew that antiskid protection is lost; use manual, modulated braking and
increase the landing distance factor per the performance supplement.

(M) Deactivate the antiskid control per AMM 32-31-00, placard the affected
control, and verify no associated hydraulic caution remains.

# MEL 32-42-02 — Autobrake RTO Mode Inoperative

Category C. Dispatch permitted for up to 10 calendar days.

(O) The RTO autobrake mode may be dispatched inoperative provided the flight crew
briefs manual maximum braking for a rejected takeoff and does not arm RTO. Normal
landing autobrake modes remain available if annunciated serviceable.

(M) Confirm the fault is isolated to the RTO mode and placard the autobrake
selector accordingly.
