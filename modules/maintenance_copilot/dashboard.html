Retrieve, validate, and cross-reference maintenance documentation — AMM, MEL, CDL, TSM — grounded strictly in the approved manuals. Every recommendation is cited; every knowledge-graph link stays unverified until an engineer confirms it. Dispatch is never automated.

Guardrails

Mandatory citation

Uncited sentences are dropped from synthesized answers — no unsourced claims about manual content.

Confidence threshold

Low-confidence retrievals are labelled and routed for mandatory manual review, never presented as settled.

Version-aware retrieval

Defaults to the current approved revision and flags any cited page that may be superseded.

Full audit trail

Every query, recommendation, and engineer confirmation is logged for regulatory traceability.
