# Annual Leave Policy

## Overview

All full-time employees are entitled to 20 days of paid annual leave per calendar year.
Leave accrues on a monthly basis and must be used within the same calendar year.
Unused leave does not carry over to the following year.

## Requesting Leave

Employees must submit leave requests at least two weeks in advance through the
HR management system. Requests require manager approval and should be scheduled
to maintain team coverage. Emergency leave may be taken with same-day notice
and must be reported to the manager and HR immediately.

## Seasonal Closures

The company observes public holidays and an annual winter closure from December
24 to December 31. Days falling within this period do not count against annual
leave entitlements. Additional closure dates are communicated annually by HR.
